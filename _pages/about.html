I am an aerospace engineer specialising in nonlinear Bayesian estimation and navigation systems. I bridge the gap between advanced mathematical theory and real-world implementation by developing and testing multiple estimation algorithms including particle flow and Gaussian mixture filters.

My research experience includes roles as a visiting student researcher at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) and the KAIST Graduate School of Mobility, focusing on applications in multi-target tracking, autonomous robotic localization, and space navigation systems.

I am interested in nonlinear estimation problems and aerospace and space applications (+ space!). I have a dream, one day I contribute to a big project that makes a real difference in the world air/space craft application.
