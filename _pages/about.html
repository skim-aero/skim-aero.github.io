Sukkeun Kim is a postdoctoral researcher at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology and an aerospace engineer specializing in nonlinear Bayesian filtering and navigation systems. He bridges the gap between advanced mathematical theory and real-world implementation by developing and testing multiple estimation algorithms including particle flow and Gaussian mixture filters.

He earned his BSc and MSc from Pusan National University and his PhD from Cranfield University. His research experience includes roles as a visiting student researcher at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) and the KAIST Graduate School of Mobility, focusing on applications in multi-target tracking, autonomous robotic localization, and space navigation systems.
